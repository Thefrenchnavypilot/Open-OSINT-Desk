# Learning path

## Beginner sequence

1. Learn the difference between a search result, a source and evidence.
2. Practice advanced search operators on non-sensitive public topics.
3. Compare an official statement with independent reporting.
4. Reverse-search a public image and find its earliest known appearance.
5. Build a source log and a dated event timeline.
6. Write a short report separating facts, statements and assessments.
7. Ask another learner to reproduce the research and identify gaps.

## Starter exercise: verify a public image

Choose a non-sensitive image from a public news article.

- Record the article URL and publication date.
- Use reverse-image search to find earlier appearances.
- Compare captions, crops and visible context.
- Check whether the image is old or from another event.
- List what can and cannot be established.
- Do not attempt to identify private people.

## Glossary
- **OSINT:** research using publicly accessible information.
- **Primary source:** material originating from the person or institution directly involved.
- **Corroboration:** independent evidence supporting a claim.
- **Geolocation:** determining where an image or event occurred.
- **Chronolocation:** estimating when an image or event occurred.
- **Attribution:** linking information or activity to a source or actor; requires careful evidence.
- **Confidence:** degree of support for a specific assessment.
