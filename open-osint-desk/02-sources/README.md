# Public sources and datasets

Prefer primary sources for what an institution or organization has formally stated. Use independent reporting to corroborate events and provide context.

## Official and intergovernmental
- [United Nations](https://www.un.org/)
- [NATO](https://www.nato.int/)
- [European Union](https://european-union.europa.eu/)
- [OSCE](https://www.osce.org/)
- [European External Action Service](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/)

## Conflict and humanitarian data
- [ACLED](https://acleddata.com/) — political violence and protest event data; methodology and access conditions should be reviewed.
- [ReliefWeb](https://reliefweb.int/) — humanitarian reports and updates.
- [UN OCHA](https://www.unocha.org/) — humanitarian coordination and situation reporting.

## Earth observation and environment
- [Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/)
- [NASA Earthdata](https://earthdata.nasa.gov/)
- [NASA FIRMS](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/)

## Research standard
For every source, document publisher, purpose, methodology, update frequency, geographic coverage, access restrictions, known bias or gaps, and last checked date.
