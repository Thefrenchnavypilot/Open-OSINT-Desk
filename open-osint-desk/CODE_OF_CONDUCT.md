# Code of Conduct

Open OSINT Desk is committed to a welcoming, respectful and evidence-focused community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and open to correction.
- Critique claims and methods, not people.
- Cite sources and distinguish evidence from opinion.
- Respect privacy, consent, applicable law and platform terms.
- Make room for beginners and different levels of expertise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, doxxing or targeted intimidation.
- Publishing private addresses, personal contact details or credentials.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, stalking or surveillance of private individuals.
- Discriminatory or abusive conduct.
- Deliberately fabricating evidence, sources or quotations.

Maintainers may edit or remove unsafe contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
