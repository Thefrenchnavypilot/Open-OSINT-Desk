# OSINT tools

A tool is only as reliable as its data, coverage and the researcher's method. Verify current pricing, access conditions and terms before use.

## Search and discovery
- [Google Advanced Search](https://www.google.com/advanced_search)
- [Bing](https://www.bing.com/)
- [OSINT Framework](https://osintframework.com/) — directory of public research resources.
- [Bellingcat Online Investigation Toolkit](https://bellingcat.gitbook.io/toolkit) — investigative tools and techniques.

## News and event data
- [GDELT](https://www.gdeltproject.org/) — global news and event data; automated extraction can contain errors.
- [Google News](https://news.google.com/) — news discovery, not a verification source by itself.

## Geospatial and satellite
- [Copernicus Browser](https://browser.dataspace.copernicus.eu/) — access to Sentinel imagery and related data.
- [NASA FIRMS](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/) — active fire information; thermal detections are not automatically evidence of a specific event.
- [Google Earth](https://earth.google.com/) — geographic context and historical imagery availability varies.

## Image and video verification
- [InVID-WeVerify](https://www.invid-project.eu/tools-and-services/invid-verification-plugin/)
- [Google Lens](https://lens.google/)
- [TinEye](https://tineye.com/)
- [ExifTool](https://exiftool.org/) — metadata inspection; metadata may be absent or altered.

## Aviation and maritime public data
- [OpenSky Network](https://opensky-network.org/) — research-oriented aircraft tracking data with coverage limitations.
- [ADS-B Exchange](https://www.adsbexchange.com/) — aircraft data; availability and completeness vary.
- [MarineTraffic](https://www.marinetraffic.com/) — AIS-based vessel information; AIS gaps do not prove concealment or absence.

## Cybersecurity context (defensive/public research only)
- [VirusTotal](https://www.virustotal.com/)
- [urlscan.io](https://urlscan.io/)
- [Shodan](https://www.shodan.io/) — use for public exposure research; do not probe systems without authorization.

**Maintenance note:** entries are an initial starter list, not a certification or endorsement. Contributors should verify each resource and add access conditions, limitations and a last-checked date before relying on it.
