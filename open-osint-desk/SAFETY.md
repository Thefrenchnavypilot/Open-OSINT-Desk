# Safety, ethics and responsible use

This project is for education, verification and research using information lawfully accessible to the public.

## Allowed focus

- Public events, institutions, policies and official statements
- Verification of public claims, images and videos
- Historical and contextual research
- Public datasets and aggregate trends
- Media literacy and source assessment

## Out of scope

- Doxxing or identifying private individuals for exposure
- Tracking a private person's movements or location
- Real-time tactical tracking that could enable targeting of people or units
- Accessing accounts, devices, networks or restricted systems without authorization
- Credential theft, phishing, malware, exploitation or evasion
- Publishing sensitive personal information or information that creates a foreseeable risk of harm

## Handling evidence

- Preserve the original URL and access date.
- Keep copies only where lawful and necessary.
- Avoid republishing graphic or sensitive material.
- Redact personal data not essential to the public-interest finding.
- Explain uncertainty, missing context and alternative explanations.
- Do not infer identity, intent or affiliation from weak indicators.

## Responsible reporting

A finding should be proportionate to the public interest, supported by evidence and reviewed for foreseeable harm before publication. When in doubt, do not publish sensitive details.
