# Open OSINT Desk

**An open, free and community-driven OSINT learning and research resource.**

Open OSINT Desk helps people learn how to find, assess, verify and responsibly communicate information from publicly available sources.

> **Mission:** make open-source research more accessible, transparent, reproducible and responsible.

## What you'll find

- Curated OSINT tools and public data sources
- Practical research and verification workflows
- Geolocation and image/video verification learning resources
- AI-assisted research prompts with human verification requirements
- Reusable report templates
- Beginner-friendly exercises and a shared glossary

## Project principles

1. **Public-interest research:** focus on events, institutions, public claims and matters of legitimate public interest.
2. **Evidence before conclusions:** preserve source URLs, publication dates, access dates and relevant context.
3. **Clear uncertainty:** distinguish confirmed facts, attributed statements, assessments and hypotheses.
4. **Reproducibility:** explain how a conclusion was reached so another researcher can check it.
5. **Privacy and safety:** do not publish personal data, private addresses, credentials, or material that enables harassment or targeting.
6. **No unauthorized access:** no intrusion, credential theft, evasion, stalking or access to restricted systems.
7. **Respectful collaboration:** welcome corrections and contributions in good faith.

## Quality labels

Each resource should include:
- Official name and URL
- Category and purpose
- Free / freemium / paid status (checked on a stated date)
- Geographic and data coverage
- Known limitations
- Date last checked
- Whether registration is required

A listing is not an endorsement. Users must independently assess legality, terms of service, reliability and suitability.

## Languages

The main project language is English to support international collaboration. French resources and translations are welcome. Keep technical terms consistent and link translations to their original document.

## Disclaimer

This repository is educational. It is not legal advice, a source of real-time intelligence, or a guarantee that a tool or dataset is complete or accurate. OSINT findings can be wrong, incomplete, delayed or misleading. Always verify important claims independently.
